🎯 Core Concepts
What is a Digital FTE?
A Digital FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) — also called AI Worker or AI Employee — is a role-based AI agent that:

Performs structured work continuously inside organizations

Follows explicit specifications and domain expertise

Operates under human oversight and accountability

Can scale instantly and work 24/7

The Three Layers of Agentic Work
General Agents — Use AI to solve problems (Mode 1)

AI Workers — Build AI that does work for you (Mode 2)

AI-Native Companies — Assemble Workers into scalable enterprises

The 10-80-10 Rule
Phase	What	Who
10%	Intent & Vision	Human defines spec, constraints, budget
80%	Execution	AI Workers compose tools, deliver outcomes
10%	Verification	Human reviews, refines, approves
🏗️ The Seven Invariants
The architectural rules of the AI-Native Company:

Human is the principal — Every chain of action originates with a human

Every human needs a delegate — Personal agent holds your context and authority

Workforce needs a management layer — Operating system for hiring, assigning, governing

Workers are role-based — Each Worker has a defined role and scope

Work is spec-driven — Clear specifications before execution

Workforce is expandable under policy — Dynamic hiring under governance

Every action is observable — Complete audit trails and accountability

🔬 The Bigger Picture
The SaaSpocalypse
Software-as-a-Service is being unbundled into:

Capabilities → Survive as APIs and MCP servers (demoted from product to function call)

System of Record → The prize (authoritative data agents must read)

UI → Dies (agents bypass screens; no one left to click)

The PC as We Know It
The app-on-OS model driven by hand through a graphical shell is becoming obsolete:

What dies: Apps as the unit of work; graphical shell; human as operator

What survives: OS as plumbing; underlying capabilities as APIs; human as source of intent and judgment

Agents as Economic Actors
Agents will soon:

Authorize payments via ACP, AP2, x402, MPP protocols

Buy compute, data, and services autonomously

Participate in markets as buyers within budget and permission envelopes
